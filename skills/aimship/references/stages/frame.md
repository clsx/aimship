# Clarify the problem

## Basis

Read the original request, background, constraints and available evidence.

## Work and result

Establish the problem, intended outcome, non-goals and assumptions worth checking. Separate known facts from hypotheses; a short section in requirements is enough.

## Continue when

The commissioned exploration has a clear objective and its uncertainty is visible. Do not force this stage when the goal is already clear.

## Decisions and blockers

A missing business objective or incompatible expected outcomes needs a user decision; independent evidence gathering may continue.

Use [the shared core](../core.md) for state, evidence and continuation rules. Record meaningful results in an artifact or referenced section; a file alone is not completion.

## Artifact contract

Use the selected PRD requirement entry. For a new PRD preserve the ordinary template headings and section order; a complete adopted PRD retains its own outline. Follow [document patterns](../document-patterns.md) and the single [artifact map](../artifact-map.json). Keep this run independent even when its document is shared. New runs need a precise result location; adopted work is not a newly executed stage.

After completing and self-checking this stage, save its result and current stop. Report the artifacts and next action, then stop. Wait for continuation without adding a document approval; final handoff instead awaits the actual acceptance decision. Failures remain unresolved until repaired and checked.
