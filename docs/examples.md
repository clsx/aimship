# Executable examples moved

The synthetic examples are developer checks. Their instructions and coverage now live in [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md#executable-examples).

For everyday use, see the [Guide](guide.md) or [中文使用指南](guide.zh-CN.md).

The guide's sign-up/login walkthrough and the README's order-export excerpts are illustrative. A real batch ends after implementation, batch review and authorized repairs; overall review is a separate saved scope after all batches close, followed by formal testing in the prepared environment.

Current-work summaries in `status.md` and the limited generated sections of `plan.md`, `review.md` and `testing.md` come from saved state. Task definitions, findings and execution evidence remain in the document body. The current workflow uses schema 5 / protocol 2; historical protocol-1 documents remain readable but their states are not migrated or continued by the new tool. See [upgrade guidance](guide.md#guide-upgrade).
